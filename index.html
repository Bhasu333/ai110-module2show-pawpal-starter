About the Event

AI CodeFest & Hackathon 2026 is an immersive weekend hacking event designed for high school and college students who want to test their skills in building intelligent technologies. Participants will form teams to design, code, and deploy innovative artificial intelligence projects using cutting-edge models and development tools.

Throughout the weekend, attendees will have access to tech workshops hosted by industry experts, covering topics from neural networks to building user-friendly LLM applications. It is the perfect opportunity to learn, network with like-minded creators, and build projects that could win major prizes.

What to Expect

Tag Line

  • Workshops: Interactive sessions on AI APIs, Python, and front-end tools.
  • Mentorship: 1-on-1 guidance from software engineers and AI developers.
  • Demos: Presenting finished projects to a panel of expert judges.
